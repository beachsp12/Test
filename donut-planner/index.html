How the math works, sources, and what is assumed

Verified against the game (Game8, Serebii, Rotom Labs / PKHeX data, and Lusamine's disassembly notes):

  • Flavor Score is the plain sum of every berry's Sweet + Spicy + Sour + Bitter + Fresh points. Nothing else feeds it.
  • Stars come from Flavor Score alone: ★ 120 · ★★ 240 · ★★★ 350 · ★★★★ 700 · ★★★★★ 960. Below 120 the donut has no star and no multiplier.
  • Level Boost = floor(sum of berry Level values × star multiplier). Donut Energy = floor(sum of Calories × multiplier). Multipliers are ×1.1, ×1.2, ×1.3, ×1.4, ×1.5 for 1 to 5 stars.
  • Slots: 3 berries at first, one more per butter upgrade, 8 max. Any berries can be combined, repeats included (8× the same berry is a normal recipe).
  • Donut type is the highest flavor (Sweet Meringue, Spicy Curry, Sour Jam, Bitter Chocolate, Fresh Cream). A tie for highest makes a Rainbow donut.
  • Flavor Powers are random, but only flavors at 120+ points can roll one. Each flavor's "budget" (1 to 9) rises at 120, 160, 200, 300, 360, 420, 525, 700, 760; budgets of 5+ can roll Level 3 powers, and 9 guarantees one on the first roll. Rainbow donuts add +1 to every flavor's budget.
  • Berry table: 66 berries (33 regular, 33 Hyper). Serebii's table and Rotom Labs' PKHeX-sourced data agree on every value.

Assumed, not verified:

  • A donut can be made with as few as 1 berry. Every source shows recipes of 3 to 8; none states a minimum, so the planner allows small recipes and you can raise the slot count if the game refuses one.
  • One guide (amiibodoctor) says stars are "the average of the berries' stars, rounded down". That contradicts Game8, Bulbapedia, and the calculator code, which all use the Flavor Score thresholds above, so this planner uses the thresholds.
